3. Device Permissions

The App requests only a small number of Android system permissions, each of which is strictly necessary for its advertised features:

  • INTERNET — allows the App to access the Internet to download wallpaper content from our catalog and to load and display ads;
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — allows the App to check your network connection status before downloading content or loading ads;
  • SET_WALLPAPER — the core permission of the App: allows applying the wallpaper you choose directly to your home screen and/or lock screen;
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — allows the App to send you notifications about new wallpaper collections. This permission is optional — it is only requested with your explicit consent, and you may decline it or revoke it at any time in your device settings;
  • AD_ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — allows access to your device's advertising identifier, a unique, user-resettable ID used by our advertising partners to show you ads that are more likely to be relevant to you. You can reset this identifier or opt out of ads personalization at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

In addition, a small number of standard support permissions may be automatically included by the Google Play services libraries bundled with the App (for example, permissions that enable receiving push notifications through Google services). These are added by the libraries themselves, do not grant access to your personal content, and involve no data collection beyond what is described in this Policy.

The App does NOT request access to your photos, media library, files, contacts, camera, microphone, or location (precise or approximate).
